## The record

This stage's artifact is published in full: **[the sample routing brief](../../examples/routing-brief-sample.md)** — a genuine run of the production contract against this case's fictional posting, auditable field by field.

What the brief decided, in short:

- **The buyer** is a lifecycle marketing team buying *campaign-decision support, not dashboard throughput* — so the bright-but-wrong tool-led read (generic data analyst, data-engineer-lite) is filed under `wrong_pool` with its cost attached: the HM can't picture that candidate in the monthly readout.
- **The ceiling** goes up before anyone is tempted: `attribution`, `churn model`, `campaign ownership`, platform administration, and seniorized phrasing are written into `cannot_borrow` / `high_risk_nouns` — properties of the role, not critiques of any candidate.
- **The calibration honesty rule** shows in the brief's own header: this run is JD-as-primary-truth, with the missing exact-lane peer recorded under `known_unknowns` instead of bluffed over.

The one rule that governs everything above: **the router never sees the resume.** The brief was written blind to candidate evidence, so "who are they hiring" could not drift toward "what can this candidate claim."

## The handoff

The brief is a binding, role-only contract. Stage 03 reads it *first* — and only then opens the candidate's evidence. If downstream work concludes the role read itself is wrong, it comes back here; nobody quietly patches their own copy.
