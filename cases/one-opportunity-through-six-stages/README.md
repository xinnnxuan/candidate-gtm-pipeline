# One opportunity, six stages — the full trace

> **Checkable artifact.** One opportunity moved through the entire Candidate GTM pipeline, stage by stage. The inputs are **fictional by construction** — the job post implicates no real company, the candidate evidence is invented, and every count inside the stage records is illustrative. The **rules are the production ones**: each stage record was produced under the same public contract it links to, so you can audit any decision against the skill that owns it.

The opportunity is the fictional posting used across this repository's examples — *Marketing Analyst, Lifecycle & Retention* at a mid-market B2B workflow-software company (full text inside [the sample routing brief](../../examples/routing-brief-sample.md)). Using one fictional opportunity everywhere is deliberate: the routing brief, the failed-and-fixed resume line, and this case all interlock, so the handoffs can be traced instead of asserted.

## The trace

| Stage | The decision made here | Record |
|---|---|---|
| 01 · Market Research | Does this opportunity deserve the next hour? | [The qualification call](01-market-research.md) |
| 02 · Audience Strategy | Who reads, decides, influences — and what may never be claimed? | [The routing brief](02-audience-strategy.md) |
| 03 · Positioning & Messaging | Which true evidence reduces this buyer's risk? | [The translation, gated](03-positioning-messaging.md) |
| 04 · Marketing Operations | Accurate, complete, ready to submit? | [The release QA](04-marketing-operations.md) |
| 05 · Lifecycle Operations | What changed, stalled, or needs action now? | [The market answers back](05-lifecycle-operations.md) |
| 06 · Revenue Analytics | Should this result change any rule? | [The loss, triaged](06-revenue-analytics.md) |

Each record keeps the same six fields: **decision · reason · artifact · handoff · human authority · write-back.** The full judgment behind each record lives in the stage's contract — the records deliberately do not restate the rules.

## What this case proves — and doesn't

It proves the **workflow's shape**: that six different judgments connect head-to-tail with explicit handoffs, that the human gates sit where the records say they sit, and that the same rules produce consistent artifacts. It does **not** prove scale or outcomes — dated volumes, funnel numbers, and their boundaries live in [the evidence ledger](../../docs/evidence-ledger.md), and the two claims are kept apart on purpose.
