## The record

This stage's artifact is published in full: **[the fail-and-fix run](../../examples/resume-draft-fail-and-fix.md)** — a draft line that violated stage 02's ceiling, the gate catching it, and the fix.

The shape of what happened:

- The tailoring session produced a fluent, JD-shaped bullet — *"Led lifecycle campaign analytics … driving retention optimization."* Every noun appears in the JD, which is exactly why it went to the gate.
- **Check 3 failed it**: the body proves *supported* readouts, not *led* campaigns; "driving optimization" claims an outcome loop one cohort view can't carry. The draft tripped the check's named fail signal verbatim — *the most JD-like sentence is also the least defensible one.*
- The gate **pointed and routed back** — it never rewrites. The fix re-translated the same true story under the ceiling (*supported, built, checked*), and the strongest defensible fact — every AI-assisted output checked against source records — became the visible one.

The discipline on display is structural, not moral: the failing line and the passing line describe the same work. What made the drift catchable was a ceiling written upstream by a stage that never saw the evidence, plus a gate that checks it at the door.

## The handoff

A graded page, its decision trace, and an operator approval. Stage 04 receives materials whose claims have already survived the gate — its job is narrower and colder: facts, fields, and the official record.
