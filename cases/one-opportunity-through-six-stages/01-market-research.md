## The record

**Coverage first.** The sweep round this posting arrived in reports its own health before any market conclusion: all sources in the batch completed, no degraded queries. (Illustrative — the point is the order: a thin round without this line is a collection audit, never a market read.)

**Full text, then judgment.** The posting survives deduplication and gets read whole. The work object is concrete: build and maintain funnel and cohort reporting for a lifecycle marketing team, keep tracking definitions honest, present monthly retention readouts. That is a real analyst loop, not a title veneer — the lane fits before any label is applied.

**The hard-constraint scan.** The full-text read checks for exactly the clauses a summary flattens: the experience ask is *1–3 years, internships count* — no hidden floor; no application quota; no eligibility clause buried in a late paragraph. Nothing disqualifying surfaces.

**The hiring relationship.** Direct employer posting its own seat — not a staffing intermediary. Recorded as a routing fact for downstream outreach.

**The call, with denominators.** The round's illustrative funnel: a batch of signals in, a handful surviving full-text reads, this one recommended — with its remaining risk stated: *the JD is self-contained but no exact-lane peer has been researched yet; stakeholder calibration is still owed.* A recommendation that hides its open questions isn't a judgment record.

## The handoff

The operator picks this opportunity from the slate. Only that confirmation creates the tracker record — and with it, the stage-02 obligation: before any positioning work, someone must answer *who actually reads, decides, or influences this hire.*
