# 02 · Audience Strategy

**What this stage is for:** making sure proof lands in front of the people who can actually evaluate or move the decision. Before any message exists, this stage answers who reads, who decides, who influences — and writes down which pool the role belongs to and what may never be claimed for it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Inputs** | One admitted opportunity: the full job description, org research, and stakeholder calibration notes — never the candidate's evidence |
| **The decision** | *Who actually reads, decides, or influences this hire?* |
| **Handoff** | A role-only routing brief — correct pool, must-prove, claim ceiling — that binds everything downstream, handed to [03 · Positioning & Messaging](../03-positioning-messaging/README.md) |
| **Human authority** | Calibration gaps are recorded, never bluffed; a flagged pool call waits for human correction |

## Skills at this stage

| Skill | Role | The judgment it owns |
|---|---|---|
| **[Audience & Routing](audience-routing/SKILL.md)** | lead | Correct pool, must-prove, and claim ceiling — written blind, before the resume is ever opened |

**繁中摘要** — 這一站在打開履歷之前先讀懂市場：這個職缺屬於哪個人才池、任何候選人都必須證明什麼、哪些字眼沒有直接證據就不准寫（claim ceiling）。路由判斷對候選人證據全盲，避免 AI 先看過亮點後把職缺「讀成」適合的樣子。產出的 routing brief 是下游所有定位與 QA 的約束契約。
