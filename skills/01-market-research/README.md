# 01 · Market Research

**What this stage is for:** keeping scarce attention on opportunities that can actually move forward. Every downstream cost — stakeholder research, routing, tailoring, release QA, follow-through — is committed the moment an opportunity is admitted, so the first stage's job is to make admission expensive to earn and cheap to audit.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Inputs** | Raw market signals: live openings captured at volume, each with its source — plus the capture's own health report |
| **The decision** | *Which opportunity deserves the next hour?* |
| **Handoff** | A qualified opportunity slate — recommended / declined with reasons and denominators. Only the operator's confirmed picks become tracked pursuits, and those move to [02 · Audience Strategy](../02-audience-strategy/README.md) |
| **Human authority** | The slate pick is never delegated; an empty slate is a legal outcome |

## Skills at this stage

| Skill | Role | The judgment it owns |
|---|---|---|
| **[Opportunity Qualification](opportunity-qualification/SKILL.md)** | lead | Full-text qualification of live openings; the stop-point where recommendation is never admission |

**Recommended start:** open [the lead contract](opportunity-qualification/SKILL.md), then watch the same judgment applied to one opportunity in [the six-stage case](../../cases/one-opportunity-through-six-stages/01-market-research.md). The wider runtime this stage compresses — sweep, intake funnel, slate — is drawn in [the staffed pipeline](../../docs/pipeline.md).
